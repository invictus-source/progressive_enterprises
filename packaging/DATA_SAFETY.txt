YOUR BUSINESS DATA IS KEPT SAFE

On a new computer, Progressive Enterprises creates a new data folder and starts
the company setup when the app is opened for the first time.

When upgrading, reinstalling, or uninstalling the application, existing business
data is not deleted. This includes the database, invoices, exports, company
settings, preferences, and backups stored in your Windows AppData folder.

Removing business data is a separate manual action and is never performed by
this installer.
